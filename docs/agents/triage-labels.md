# Triage labels

- `needs-triage`: not yet classified or reproduced.
- `needs-info`: blocked on a concrete fact or reproduction from the reporter.
- `ready-for-agent`: scoped, testable, and safe for autonomous implementation.
- `ready-for-human`: needs maintainer judgment, credentials, publication, or an
  external policy decision.
- `wontfix`: intentionally outside scope or rejected with a recorded reason.

Move an issue to exactly one of these workflow labels after triage. Standards
and severity labels may be added independently.
